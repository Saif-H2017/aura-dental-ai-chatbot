🔒 Privacy Policy & Patient Confidentiality

Last updated: August 2026 • General Dental Council (GDC) & NHS Compliant

1. Data Controller Notice
Aura Dental Studio London ("we", "our") is committed to protecting patient confidentiality and processing personal data in strict compliance with the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and UK GDPR.

2. Information We Collect
When you use our 24/7 AI Receptionist or online appointment booking system, we collect your full name, phone number, email address, and clinical symptom notes to schedule consultations and evaluate emergency triage requirements.

3. Use of AI Assistance
Intake queries processed by our AI receptionist are encrypted in transit and used strictly for appointment triage and clinical intake. We do not sell or monetize patient data.

4. Your Rights
You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal records at any time by contacting our Marylebone reception team at privacy@auradental.co.uk.

📋 Terms of Service & Clinical Intake Guidelines

Aura Dental Studio Clinical Policies

1. AI Triage Limitation
Our 24/7 AI Receptionist provides preliminary triage guidance based on NHS emergency protocols. It does not replace emergency medical response. For acute severe trauma, uncontrolled bleeding, or breathing difficulty, call 999 or attend A&E immediately.

2. Appointment Bookings & Cancellations
Confirmed appointments reserve dedicated time with Dr. Alexander Wright. We request at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling.

3. Private Care Pricing
Treatment costs discussed during AI chat are estimated ranges. Final treatment plans and fees are confirmed during your clinical consultation.

🛡️ GDPR & Data Handling Policy

UK GDPR Article 13 & 14 Disclosure

All patient booking records and Google Calendar integrations utilize 256-bit SSL encryption. Data is stored on secure UK/EU server nodes compliant with healthcare data security standards.

2. Retention Period
Clinical triage notes and booking histories are retained in accordance with UK health record regulations for a minimum period of 8 years following clinical consultation.
